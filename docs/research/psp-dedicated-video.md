### Spoken acronym expansion

**Precision Swing Point is the only spoken long-form expansion attested in the dedicated video (and reconfirmed in the TTrades guest lecture).**

Garrett opens Pt.1 by naming the concept and binding the short form:

> “…and that is with the concept **precision swing points or PSP for shorts**. This is a concept founded by trader day. … Today I will be showing you how I use **precision swing point**.”  
> — [iXRQg-OpO6Y](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXRQg-OpO6Y)

He then defines it by asking the long form explicitly:

> “So **what is a precision swing point**? It is a swing point, right? But it is not just a swing point. It is also a [crack in] correlation, right? … The difference in closes is the [crack in] correlation, right? They're going to **close in opposing directions**. … Asset one closes bullish. Asset two, the correlated market closes bearish. It's as simple as that.”  
> — [iXRQg-OpO6Y](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXRQg-OpO6Y)

*(Auto-transcript often renders “crack in” as “Kraken”; surrounding lines also say “cracking correlation,” matching Garrett’s usual SMT language.)*

Closing of the same video again uses the long form:

> “…hopefully this video helped you guys understand **precision swing point**. Again, something that I use literally every single day…”  
> — [iXRQg-OpO6Y](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXRQg-OpO6Y)

Supporting primary (guest lecture on TTrades):

> “…we're just adding in a **precision swing point**. … It is where between a correlated markets they **close in opposite directions**.”  
> — [3eVxTV_7L2U](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eVxTV_7L2U) (Garrett on TTrades)

### Operational definition (from Pt.1, synthesized from Garrett’s own wording)

Not a new NewGxT product rule — primary paraphrase only:

1. **PSP = swing formation + crack-in-correlation via opposite closes** on correlated assets (same candle / swing window).
2. Variants he teaches in Pt.1: plain PSP; **two-stage PSP** (PSP + SMT between C1–C2 or C2–C3); **continuation PSP**; **strength switch PSP** (sweeping/strong asset closes reverse polarity — concept he claims as his packaging).
3. Must sit in **framework / narrative** (IRL↔ERL, order-pairing ranges, phases of price) — not pattern-traded alone.
4. Credits **Trader Day** (Quarterly Theory) for the concept origin; credits **Jacob Speculates** for related “precision candle” framing; distinguishes his fractal/framework application from full QT.
